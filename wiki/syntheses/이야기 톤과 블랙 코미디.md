# Story Tone and Black Comedy

The preferred tone is dense scene-level prose with tragedy treated seriously and black comedy emerging from institutional absurdity, monstrous normalcy, and sincere but warped logic.

## Principles

- Do not make tragedy frivolous.
- Let comedy arise from how powerful people administratively normalize horrors.
- Characters can be monstrous and still act with everyday sincerity.
- Details should be preserved rather than compressed into generic fantasy summary.

## Examples

- Nerine's anger at vampire Acerorion focuses on wasted utility under Neblade, not ordinary moral horror.
- Magic Territory treats vampire attacks as injury crimes, then assigns aftercare to the ritual's creator.
- White Swan Tower is known as a severe noble prison but functions like a luxury resort-prison.
- Chronica sincerely wants vineyard work but is repeatedly dragged into grimoire and vampire administration.
- Szas can be objectively correct while appearing to study mass death as an interesting phenomenon.

## Prose Style

The user prefers "real novel style" at scene level: tighter sentences, dialogue, embodied reactions, and black comedy flavor from the original material.
