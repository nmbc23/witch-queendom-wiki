# Mubeil, Acerorion, Dimari, and the Jewel Sword Arc

This arc begins as scholarly exchange and social comedy and ends as a failed youth of equals.

## Summary

Aika Desmone enters Liminaris through the fragment body Mubeil Aiki Lau and becomes entangled with Acerorion Liminaris, a lord traumatized by Nerine's rejection and political manipulation. Acerorion finds in Mubeil someone powerful yet apparently unconnected to Nerine's backdoors. Mubeil, Acerorion, Dimari, and Meibell collaborate around dimensional foam and the Kaleidoscope Jewel Sword.

The research succeeds enough to prove the theory and create a blueprint, but not enough to build the artifact with current technology. The team is emotionally significant precisely because it briefly lets Aika interact with near-equals outside the burdens of queenship.

Sierra's later explanation reframes the exchange as Aika's attempt to regain dimensional foam control after losing Haisia's support. Mubeil behaves at first like an indifferent graduate exchange researcher, but that very lack of political hunger attracts Acerorion.

## Collapse

Acerorion pursues forbidden vampire magic to become strong enough to resist Nerine and not be replaced. She drinks Mubeil/Aika's blood, learns Aika's true identity, and becomes obsessively happy. Nerine arrives and destroys Acerorion with powers far beyond Aika's first estimate.

For Mubeil and Dimari, the Jewel Sword result is a half-success: the route is proven even if decades of technology are missing. For Acerorion, who wants a present escape from Nerine and fears Mubeil will be taken into Nerine's service, the same result feels like failure. This emotional mismatch pushes her toward Chronica's vampire technique.

Meibell and Acerorion's will allow Mubeil to escape. Dimari's reluctance to kill Meibell becomes the moral gap through which Aika survives.

## Aftermath

The Jewel Sword blueprint survives, but the living collaboration is broken. Later Aika gives the blueprint to Leona Haway, making the lost Liminaris youth into a future political weapon.

## Alternate IF: A Pleasant Nightmare

The IF route `[[syntheses/pleasant-nightmare-if-route|A Pleasant Nightmare]]` stretches this arc without truly escaping it.

- Izlea's hesitation gives Acerorion and Mubeil more time in the cellar.
- Meibell's anonymous letters and Dimari's investigation move Nerine toward deep review and an "excursion" that functions as undeclared military intervention.
- The route still converges on exposure and collapse; its final claim is that they walked a different road only to arrive at the same place.
- The worst branch goes further and argues that the clean separation of the mainline was in some sense more merciful.
