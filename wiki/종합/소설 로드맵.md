# Current Novel Roadmap

## Major Arcs

1. Source cleanup and reconstruction: remove AI reactions/annotations from the original novel and preserve details.
2. Liminaris exchange: Mubeil/Aika enters Liminaris, gains Acerorion's trust, works with Dimari and Meibell.
3. Northern lake incident: Sushie El/Sierra fragment, monsters, dimensional foam sword prototype, and Aika's noble rescue performance.
4. Jewel Sword research: dimensional foam, Planescape, Liminaris facilities, Dimari tuning, Meibell shards.
5. Acerorion's vampire fall: Nerine trauma, forbidden ritual, Aika's identity exposed, obsessive blood bond.
6. Nerine suppression and escape: Acerorion's death, Meibell vs Dimari, Aika's hidden team and black magic car.
7. Aftermath: Liminaris succession, false bakery rumor, Leona receives blueprint, Dimari's loss.
8. Chronica's vampire legacy: blood-core theory, Kamaso catastrophe, Magic Territory settlement, vampire regulation.
9. Szas/Laroka/Kamila future rebellion: Szas's hidden theory becomes a later great-demon reconstruction.
10. Sierra Serasie expansion: founder of magic, teacher of Szas and Laroka, throne and letter-chain imagery.

- Chronica learning that her successful Magic Territory settlement has become a defection precedent.
- Sierra teaching Szas and Laroka, with the letter chains implying magic originates from her.
- Naksha confronting Szas after Kamila's territory is lost.
- Meijers realizing Forced Victory might not be loyal to her.
