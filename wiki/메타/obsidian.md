# Obsidian

Obsidian is a local markdown knowledge-base application suggested by the source as a natural interface for browsing an LLM-maintained wiki.

## Role In This Wiki Pattern

- Obsidian can act as the human reading and navigation layer.
- Codex acts as the writer and maintainer of markdown files.
- The graph view helps reveal hubs, isolated pages, and relationship structure.
- Plugins such as Dataview and Marp can add dynamic tables or slide workflows if the wiki grows in that direction.

## Notes

This page is about Obsidian's role in the LLM Wiki workflow, not a general guide to Obsidian.
