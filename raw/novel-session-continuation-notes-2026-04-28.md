## Chronica's successful settlement in Magic Territory

- Chronica Criella adapted to Magic Territory better than Queen Territory expected.
- This was not because Magic Territory was easy, but because it was more short-staffed than it appeared.
- Magic Territory had many strong magicians, talented magicians, and reckless magicians, but fewer people who could read dangerous grimoires without collapsing, classify them, run a cult/temple organization, and handle paperwork.
- Chronica's old Queen Territory experience as a lesser demon, monastery manager, vineyard operator, grimoire editor, and cult administrator became unexpectedly valuable.
- Her successful settlement created a dangerous precedent: Queen Territory personnel began to think that defection to Magic Territory might be viable.
- For Queen Territory, Chronica became less a one-off exile and more a proof of concept: if even a politically damaged lesser demon could be useful there, other displaced nobles, scholars, and minor demons might also defect.
- Chronica herself did not intend to become a path for others; she only fled to survive.

- Correction: Sierra's ears should be human-like, not pointed or elven.
- Sierra currently has no staff. Magic is not represented by a tool but by language chains extending from her.
