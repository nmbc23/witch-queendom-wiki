# Chronica Queen/Mubeil Appearance Clarification

Date: 2026-04-29
Source: user clarification in Codex chat

Canonical clarification:

- Chronica knows the queen's official appearance, but only from video.
- Chronica has never actually met the queen or seen her in person.
- Mubeil's appearance is completely different from the queen's official/video appearance.

- Chronica's later suspicion/recognition should come from scale, aura, queen-territory clues, treatment context, and the absurd level of compensation/secrecy, not from matching Mubeil's face to the queen.
