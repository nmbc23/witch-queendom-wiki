## Scope

This source bundle captures the long-running Codex session about the user's original fantasy novel arc centered on [[entities/aika-desmone-mubeil-aiki-lau|Aika/Mubeil]], [[entities/acerorion-liminaris|Acerorion]], [[entities/dimari-hyuri|Dimari]], [[entities/meibell-nast|Meibell]], the [[concepts/만화경 보석검|Kaleidoscope Jewel Sword]], [[entities/chronica-criella|Chronica]], [[entities/kamaso-kamila|Kamaso Kamila]], [[entities/szas-sreya|Szas Sreya]], [[entities/laroka|Laroka]], [[entities/meijers-desmone-medalicia|Meijers]], and [[entities/sierra-serasie|Sierra Serasie]].

## Key Claims

- The early project goal was to remove AI reactions/annotations from a source novel file and reassemble it as a single coherent novel without losing detail.
- The story's core tragic-comic arc follows Queen Aika, disguised as Mubeil Aiki Lau, during her stay in Liminaris and her relationships with Acerorion, Dimari, and Meibell.
- Acerorion's trauma began when Nerine turned cold after Acerorion asked about the ceiling orbs and called her "mother" for the last time.
- Acerorion leaned on Mubeil because Mubeil seemed powerful but unconnected to Nerine's hidden control network.
- The Kaleidoscope Jewel Sword is a collaborative artifact whose completion requires Mubeil/Aika's dimensional foam work, Acerorion's Liminaris dimensional foundation, Dimari's precise circuit/time tuning, and Meibell/Leona's later involvement.
- Acerorion's vampire transformation comes from Chronica's originally romantic blood-sharing ritual being weaponized into blood-core growth.
- Nerine defeats Acerorion with stored/borrowed powers that reveal a much greater threat than her apparent personal class suggests.
- Mubeil escapes with help from Meibell, Chronica, and Aika's hidden Queen Territory companions, leaving behind a false public narrative.
- Chronica's vampire magic and blood-core theory later connect to the Kamaso Kamila catastrophe, Magic Territory vampire management, and Szas/Laroka's later resurrection of Kamila as a true great demon.
- Szas is defined by correct but inhuman reasoning: she sees catastrophe as structure and rare experimental possibility.
- Laroka's essence is a mass-bearing illusion; her old-woman and girl forms are deliberate appearances.
- Sierra Serasie is now defined as the founder of magic and teacher of Szas and Laroka, seated on a throne and bound by letter chains that originate from herself.
- The chronological prompt extraction file contains 401 ordered prompt/source fragments and is the best in-folder source for checking scene order, original user-side wording, and late future-thread additions.

## Source Reliability Notes

- The handoff file is a consolidated session summary, not the original prose source. It is useful for continuity and future Codex sessions.
- The prompt extraction file is more granular than the handoff, but it is still a reconstruction aid rather than a clean final manuscript.
- Some items may need reconciliation with the original novel file if exact chronology or wording matters.
- Sierra Serasie has two context clusters: earlier, a Sierra/Sushie fragment appears in the Liminaris lake arc; later, Sierra Serasie is defined as Szas and Laroka's teacher and founder of magic. Treat this as an open continuity point until resolved.
