# Chronica Devotees / Magic Territory Interest Clarification

Date: 2026-04-29
Source: user clarification in Codex chat

Canonical clarification:

- When Chronica went to the Magic Territory, she was prepared to speak solemnly and ask that her devotees' lives be guaranteed even if something happened to her.
- Chronica expected the negotiation to be about responsibility, punishment, and protecting the people who followed her.
- The Magic Territory, however, was mostly interested in Chronica herself.
- Her devotees mattered less to the Magic Territory than the rare value of Chronica as a person who could read dangerous grimoires, classify knowledge, manage a religious organization, handle administration, and survive supernatural trouble.
- This should frame the scene as both sincere and darkly comic: Chronica arrives ready for martyr-like responsibility, but the receiving side is evaluating her as valuable specialist personnel.
